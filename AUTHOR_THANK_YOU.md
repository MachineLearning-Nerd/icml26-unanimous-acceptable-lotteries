# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Davin Choo, Paul W. Goldberg, and Nicholas Teh for making
*Learning Unanimously Acceptable Lotteries via Queries* and its source
materials available. The paper’s separation of membership-query algorithms,
exact finite certificates, lower-bound constructions, and prediction-sensitive
procedures made a careful claim-by-claim audit possible.

This repository is an independent reproduction and audit. It records evidence
and limitations for the named claims and does not imply review, endorsement, or
approval by the authors.
