# Limitations and deviations

The empirical family uses nested coordinate constraints to realize controlled record counts exactly. It is not a distributional model of real stakeholder predictions. The theorem itself defines quality operationally through the run, so this construction tests the exact parameter rather than a proxy.

The `R=0` case is reported under the paper's separate `n`-query footnote, not forced into a multiplicative formula that would incorrectly evaluate to zero.

Offline linear-program computation remains outside membership-query counts, matching the paper.
